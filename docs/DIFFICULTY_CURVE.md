# Campaign difficulty curve

The official campaign uses a deliberate difficulty progression rather than allowing isolated level spikes to define the experience.

- Levels 1–15: `easy`. These teach and combine mechanics without requiring extreme execution.
- Levels 16–23: `medium`. These are the main mastery band and should gradually increase commitment, precision and route complexity.
- Levels 24–25: `hard`. These are the pre-finale challenges. A future level may use `extreme` only after a hard level and only when that escalation is intentional.

Content validation must be used as feedback on level geometry, not as a reason to loosen the mechanical validator. An isolated `extreme` result in levels 1–23 is a content-design defect and should be corrected by changing the level geometry while preserving the real movement physics.

When creating future campaign content, first establish the intended difficulty band, then shape platform spacing, width and horizontal displacement to fit it. Re-run the authoritative mechanical validator after every geometry change.
