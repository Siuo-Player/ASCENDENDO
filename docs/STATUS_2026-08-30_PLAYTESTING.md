# ASCENDENDO — Status: Human Playtesting

**Date:** 2026-08-30

## Current evidence

Deterministic Vulkan capture is now exercised at:

```text
3 levels × 3 viewport classes = 9 real captures
```

The captures prove framebuffer creation and PPM readback for 16:9, 4:3 and 21:9. They do not establish human readability, camera feel, route comprehension, goal salience or difficulty balance.

## Current decision

No camera, rendering, level-geometry or difficulty change is justified by the deterministic captures alone.

The next empirical step is a controlled human playtesting pilot using:

- fixed current campaign content;
- the three validated viewport classes where available;
- short repeatable tasks;
- measurable observations plus separate qualitative reports.

## Promotion boundary

Human observations should only become a production work package when a repeatable observation identifies a specific property and player impact that can be isolated and retested.

## Studies reconciliation

The available `PROJECT-STUDIES/ASCENDENDO` material does not currently contain a dedicated human-playtesting protocol. Therefore this protocol is an operational proposal based on the current ASCENDENDO evidence boundary, not a claim extracted from an existing Study.
